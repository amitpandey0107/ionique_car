A Private Lounge without limits

The Mercedes-Benz Sprinter offers exceptional space and versatility. At ionique, we transform this generous platform into a fully bespoke passenger environment — designed for executive transport, professional limousine services and high-end group travel.

Soft ambient lighting, tailored upholstery and carefully configured seating layouts create a refined atmosphere that feels closer to a private jet cabin than a traditional vehicle interior.

With modular configurations ranging from four to seven seats in the rear passenger compartment, the Sprinter adapts to your operational or personal requirements.

Modular Seating

Flexibility meets uncompromising comfort.

4 to 7 Seat Configurations

Choose the layout that suits your needs — from exclusive executive lounge arrangements to high-capacity VIP transport

Fully Adjustable Seating

Electrically adjustable seats with heating, ventilation and multi-stage massage functions ensure exceptional comfort for every passenger

Lounge, Conference or Travel Mode

Programmable seating layouts allow seamless transitions between relaxation, business meetings or entertainment-focused journeys.

Soundproof Partition Wall with 50" Display

Discretion and immersion are central to the ionique Sprinter experience. A slim, fully soundproof partition wall separates driver and passengers, creating a serene and confidential rear cabin environment.

50” 4K Integrated Screen

A large-format ultra-high-definition display is seamlessly built into the partition wall, delivering a true cinema-level experience on the move

Smart Glass Technology

The glass partition can switch from transparent to opaque instantly, offering privacy at the touch of a button

Optional Rear 32” Screen

For extended cabin layouts, an additional 32” rear display can be installed to ensure optimal viewing from every seating position

Always Entertained

An immersive multimedia environment transforms every journey into a premium experience.

ionique Bespoke Audio

Precision-tuned acoustics provide rich, balanced sound for music, film and presentations.

Streaming & Media Integration

Integrated streaming devices including Apple TV or other preferred platforms.

Smart connectivity

Direct device integration for gaming consoles, laptops and presentation systems

Designed for professional travel

The ionique Sprinter is engineered with professional use in mind.

Triple-Level Luggage Compartment

A dedicated luggage area, intelligently divided into three levels, provides exceptional storage capacity. Ideal for:

  • Larger groups

  • Airport transfers

  • Professional taxi services

  • Executive limousine operators

This structured luggage system keeps belongings organised, secure and easily accessible without compromising passenger comfort

Multimedia connected

Travel time becomes productive time.

5G Mobile Router

High-speed mobile internet for uninterrupted connectivity.

Starlink Integration

Satellite-based internet powered by Starlink ensures reliable global coverage, even in remote regions

Integrated Workstations

Connect your laptop or utilise integrated Windows or Mac systems for meetings, presentations and collaboration.

The mobile Executive environment

Our Sprinter conversions can be equipped with:

  • USB-C docking stations

  • Integrated webcams and conference microphones

  • Wireless charging solutions

  • Power outlets and hidden cable management

  • Fold-out working tables

Whether transporting executives, hosting mobile meetings or delivering premium passenger experiences, the cabin adapts seamlessly to your purpose.

A First-class experience for every passenger

The ionique Sprinter is more than a vehicle conversion.

It is a purpose-built VIP transport solution — combining space, modular flexibility and refined luxury in one commanding presence.

For private owners, professional operators and executive fleets alike, this is travel without compromise.
