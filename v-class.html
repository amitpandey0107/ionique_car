A Private Lounge on the Move

Step into a cabin designed as a refined lounge rather than a traditional vehicle interior. Soft contours, generous space and a calm, tailored atmosphere transform every journey into a moment of quiet luxury.

Lounge mode

Settle into exceptionally crafted seating with fully programmable layouts. Effortlessly transition between lounge, work or theatre mode, tailored precisely to your preferred setting.

Beverages on
the go

Enjoy perfectly prepared hot or chilled refreshments at any moment, thanks to the integrated coffee machine and generously sized refrigerator — always within reach.

Heated / Cooled
Massage seats

Our electrically adjustable seats come equipped with heating, ventilation and multi-stage massage functions as standard. Each setting can be individually configured to create your ideal level of comfort.

Always Entertained

An immersive entertainment experience, seamlessly integrated into the cabin. Designed to make every journey as engaging as the destination itself.

40” 4K Screen

A discreetly integrated ultra-high-definition screen delivering cinema-level clarity on the move.

Exceptional audio

Rich, balanced acoustics that elevate music, film and conversation alike.

Smart connectivity

Effortless access to streaming, gaming and presentations through advanced media integration.

Soundproof Partition Wall

Discretion is at the heart of the cabin’s design. A remarkably slim, fully soundproof partition wallseparates driver and passengers, creating a serene and private rear lounge environment. Integratedvseamlessly within this elegant structure is a motorised 4K display, which rises effortlessly whenvdesired and remains entirely concealed when not in use.

For added versatility, the glass partition is equipped with advanced smart glass technology, allowing it to shift from transparent to opaque at the touch of a button. Whether open and connected or secluded and confidential, the space adapts instantly — offering privacy without compromising refinement.

Multimedia Connected

Being on the move should never mean being disconnected from your work. Our vehicle transforms travel time into productive time, offering a fully equipped mobile office designed for modern professionals.

Integrated PC

Connect your own laptop or use the integrated Windows or Mac workstation to handle important tasks, join video calls, attend meetings, and collaborate seamlessly — all while on the road.

Always connected

Powered by state-of-the-art 5G mobile routers and Starlink satellite integration, you benefit from fast, reliable internet connectivity wherever your journey takes you.

Customized Exterior

The exterior is defined by a bespoke two-tone paint finish, carefully curated to enhance both presence and proportion. Redesigned headlights introduce a sharper, more contemporary character, while subtle ionique signature badging on the bonnet and trunk reinforces its exclusive identity. The result is a silhouette that feels both commanding and elegantly composed

ionique Forged rims

Our custom-forged ionique wheels are engineered for both strength and refinement. Precisioncrafted and perfectly balanced, they complete the vehicle’s stance with confidence and sophistication, adding a final signature touch to the overall design.
