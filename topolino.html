Customized Exterior

Every ionique Topolino is finished in a bespoke single or two-tone exterior paint and carries the ionique front and rear in paint badge. The exterior reflects the style and taste of you and distinguishes itself from the generic color and styling of the Topolino. Choose from thirteen Italian-inspired colours of the Collezione Colori and combine them exactly the way you like.

ionique forged alloy wheels

16 inch forged wheels with a turbine-blade design, created exclusively for the Topolino. Machined spokes and a polished outer rim give the smallest ionique a signature stance, finished with the ionique centre cap.

Night package

All grey plastic exterior parts are painted and finished in deep black, giving the Topolino a sportier, fresher look.

A tailored cabin

Open the door and the Topolino becomes a ionique. Finest Italian Nappa leather wraps the seats and door cards, while the ceiling is tailored in leather and Alcantara. Soft ambient light sets the mood, and smart glass turns the panoramic roof opaque at the touch of a button. Every detail is crafted to the standard of a far larger luxury car, in a city car that fits anywhere.

Ceiling and pillars construction

The standard Topolino leaves its bare metal frame exposed. ionique designed an all-new ceiling and side pillars that conceal it completely, creating a seamless, finished cabin that also houses many of the ionique features.

Included options
  • Ceiling and pillars construction and upholstery in leather
  • Ceiling upholstery in Alcantara
  • Leather handles on ceiling
  • Pillars include speakers, ambient lights and reading lights

Ambient lighting & reading lights

After dark, the cabin deserves warmth. Ambient lighting along the ceiling glows in 7 different colours, each selectable through the touch infotainment screen, while individual reading lights give every seat its own light.

Included options
  • Ambient lighting with 7 different colours
  • Individual reading lights

Smart glass

Technical enhancements

Optional upgrades that take the Topolino further, faster and keep it comfortable all year round.

Additional battery package

Double the battery capacity, from 5,4 kWh to 10,8 kWh, for a range of up to 150 km.

Power performance VCU

A recalibrated vehicle control unit raises the top speed from 45 km/h to up to 70 km/h.

Webasto 2KW diesel heater

A Webasto 2 kW heater keeps the cabin warm and the glass clear in cold weather, without drawing on the driving battery.
