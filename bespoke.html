World of bespoke

Fully customized. Without bounderies.

Custom colors

Our curated palette offers up to 50 standard colours, carefully selected across a range of tones and shades to meet the highest expectations. While each colour stands beautifully on its own, we understand that true individuality goes beyond a predefined selection. Through our bespoke programme, we offer the creation of fully custom colours. Using a reference point of your choice, we develop unique, one-of-one finishes for exterior paint or interior leather—crafted exclusively for you.

Tailer-made

Much like a perfectly tailored suit, our bespoke programme is entirely made to measure. We take the time to understand your vision, refine every detail, and translate your expectations into a precise and personal result.

Premium material textures

Beyond colour, material texture plays a defining role in shaping both aesthetic and experience. The interior of your vehicle is not only something you see, but something you continuously touch and feel. We elevate this sensory experience by combining bespoke colour development with carefully selected materials, textures, and finishes. Creating interiors that feel as distinctive as they look.

Infinite
possibilities

At ionique, we believe that true luxury is the freedom to create without limitation. Our bespoke conversion programme is not simply a process. It is a collaborative journey where your ideas are explored, refined, and ultimately transformed into reality.
From the very first consultation, we work closely with you to understand your tastes, inspirations, and ambitions. Whether it is a subtle refinement or a complete reimagination of a vehicle, every element, and no detail too small.
We do not design for our clients; we design with them. You become the creative force, while our artisans, designers, and engineers bring your vision to life through exceptional craftsmanship and technical expertise.
The result is not merely a customised vehicle, but a truly one-of-one creation that reflects your identity and stands apart as a statement of individuality.
Supported by a global network of specialist partners and suppliers, we source the finest materials and most exclusive finishes from around the world.
This allows us to deliver vehicles that are not only unique, but uncompromising in quality, innovation, and presence. At ionique, there are no boundaries, only possibilities waiting to be realised.
