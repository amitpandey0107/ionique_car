our purpose

We have a clear mission: to disrupt luxury mobility by genuinely listening to people and transforming vehicles into human-centric spaces for living and working, not just travelling. At ionique, we blend advanced technology with natural materials to create a refined atmosphere: a comfortable luxury cocoon that remains serene and beautifully silent. Every line, surface, and stitch is intentional because true luxury isn’t loud. It’s considered.

made for you

Our human-centric approach is reflected in every step of the journey. From the very first contact to the delivery of your bespoke vehicle, we listen closely and guide you throughout the entire process. At ionique, you are the creative force — challenging us to craft something truly extraordinary. No idea is too bold, and no challenge too complex. If you are shaping the future of mobility and believe great design begins with people, let’s meet.

base platform

We build our interior concepts on the Mercedes-Benz V-Class platform, chosen for its exceptional balance of space, comfort, and versatility.

Exterior

Exterior refinements may be subtle, but they make a powerful statement. While every ionique begins with the interior, the exterior defines its presence and distinction. From refined two-tone paint finishes and electric side steps to carpet light projection and bespoke ionique wheels, each enhancement is designed to elevate style, character, and exclusivity.

Interior

Only the driver’s seat, doors, and dashboard remain. Everything else is removed, allowing us to rebuild the interior entirely from scratch.
Every component is designed and crafted in-house, tailored specifically to this platform. The result is a completely reimagined cabin—elevated to deliver unmatched comfort and luxury.

The build-up

Piece by piece, our interiors take shape.
Each element is crafted and perfected by our engineers and artisans.
Once assembled, they create a seamless and luxurious cabin environment that is uniquely ionique.

Born of a new ionique

The result is a bespoke interior that enhances every moment of travel.
Created in collaboration with our clients, this cocoon of luxury and privacy becomes a true one-of-one ionique—ready for the journey ahead.
